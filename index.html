🧠 Mind Reader

Think of a whole number and keep it secret. The computer asks mathematical yes/no questions — never “Is it X?” and never higher/lower. It only names a number once exactly one number fits your answers.

ROUND 01 1–100 100 possible numbers
How to play
  1. Pick a range, think of one whole number in it, and keep it in your head. Never type it anywhere.
  2. Press “I have my number”, then answer each question with Yes, No or Not sure.
  3. Not sure is safe: it narrows nothing and is marked as uncertain in your list.
  4. Made a mistake? Use the Yes / No / Not sure buttons on any earlier answer to fix it, or press Undo. The count of possible numbers is recalculated instantly.
  5. When exactly one number fits, the computer names it and asks you to confirm. A mistaken “not my number” click can be undone.
